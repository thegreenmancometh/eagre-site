Delete your data

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Eagre keeps your videos, clips and sign-ins on your own computer, so almost everything can be deleted by you, right away. Here is how to remove all of it.

1. Disconnect your accounts in Eagre

In Eagre's Settings, disconnect each YouTube, TikTok or Instagram account you connected. This deletes that account's sign-in tokens from your computer.

2. Remove Eagre's access on each platform

  • YouTube / Google: go to myaccount.google.com/permissions, choose Eagre, and remove access.
  • TikTok: in the TikTok app, go to Profile, Settings and privacy, Security, Apps and services permissions, choose Eagre, and remove access.
  • Instagram: in Instagram, go to Settings, Website permissions, Apps and websites, choose Eagre, and remove it.

Eagre does not keep posted Instagram videos: each one is deleted from temporary storage once Instagram has collected it, and within 24 hours at the latest.

3. Delete Eagre's files from your computer

Uninstall Eagre from Windows (Settings, Apps, Installed apps, Eagre, Uninstall), then delete Eagre's folder and your output folder if you no longer want your clips. This removes all projects, clips, transcripts, settings and sign-ins.

4. Ask us to delete anything we hold

The only personal data we may hold ourselves is your support emails and, if you bought a paid plan, your order details (email address, plan and licence key). To have these deleted, email hello@eagre.app with the subject "Delete my data" from the email address you used. We will confirm within one month, usually much sooner. Polar, which sold you the plan, must keep some order records for tax law; you can also contact Polar directly.
