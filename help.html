Questions

Is Eagre free?

There is a free plan with limits, and paid plans billed monthly or yearly for more clips and features. You can see what your plan includes, and what is left this month, in Settings > Your plan.

Do my videos get uploaded to your servers?

No. Eagre runs on your own PC, and your videos, clips and sign-ins stay there. A clip only leaves your PC when you post it to YouTube, TikTok or Instagram yourself. See the privacy policy.

Which streams can I clip?

Twitch, YouTube and Kick links, or any video file on your PC. A stream link has to be one you can watch without signing in. Only clip videos you have the right to use, such as your own streams or streams you clip with the streamer's permission.

How long does it take?

Finding the moments in a one-hour stream takes about 5 minutes on a PC with an NVIDIA RTX card, and around half an hour on a PC without one. Making a clip after that takes a minute or less. See PC requirements.

Do I need a graphics card?

No, but it helps a lot. Without an NVIDIA card everything runs on the processor, which works but is slower.

Where are my clips saved?

In a folder on your PC. Settings > This PC shows which one. Finished clips are also listed under Clips in the app.

Can I use Eagre on more than one PC?

Yes. One licence key works on 2 PCs, for example a desktop and a laptop. A paid plan keeps working for 14 days without internet.

How do I update Eagre?

Settings > Updates > Check for updates. Your settings, licence, projects and clips are kept.

How do I cancel or get a refund?

You can cancel any time; your plan keeps working until the end of the period you paid for. Email hello@eagre.app about refunds.

How do I remove Eagre?

Open Windows Settings > Apps, find Eagre and click Uninstall. To also delete your data, see Delete your data.

Common problems

Windows says "Windows protected your PC" or "unknown publisher"

Click More info, then Run anyway. The installer isn't signed with a paid certificate yet, so Windows doesn't recognise it. If your antivirus blocks the installer, allow it once.

A part of the first start fails

  • Press Try again. Downloads carry on where they stopped, so nothing is downloaded twice.
  • Most failures are a dropped internet connection. Check you are online, and if you use a VPN, try without it.
  • Check you have about 25 GB of free disk space.
  • The writing AI fails to install: install Ollama yourself from ollama.com, then press Try again. You can also skip this part: Eagre still finds moments and makes clips without it.
  • Still stuck? Email hello@eagre.app with the message the page shows.

Eagre doesn't open, or the page says it can't connect

Eagre's small black window must stay open while you use the app: closing it closes Eagre. Open Eagre again from its icon. If it still doesn't open, restart your PC and try once more.

A stream link doesn't work

  • Check the stream plays in your browser without signing in. Subscriber-only, private and deleted videos can't be clipped.
  • Twitch deletes old broadcasts after a while, so very old links stop working.
  • If the link still fails, download the video and drop the file into Eagre instead.

Finding moments is very slow

Run Settings > This PC > Check speed to see what your PC can do. On a slow PC, start long streams and come back later, or let it run overnight. Closing games and other heavy programs while it works helps.

"Speech-to-text isn't installed"

Part of the first start didn't finish. Close Eagre and open it again: the progress page comes back and finishes the missing parts.

YouTube posting doesn't work

  • "Access blocked" when signing in to Google: in Google Cloud, add your Google account as a test user on the OAuth consent screen (step 4 of Connect YouTube).
  • Saving the Google file fails: make sure you made an OAuth client of type Desktop app, and pasted everything in the JSON file.
  • The upload stops with a quota error: YouTube limits how many videos a new Google project can upload each day. Try again the next day.
  • To start over, disconnect the channel in Settings > Connected accounts and link it again.

Running out of disk space

Long streams take a lot of room while they are scanned. Move finished clips you have already posted off this drive, and keep at least 20 GB free.

Still need help?

Email hello@eagre.app. Tell us what you were doing, what the error message says and the verdict from Settings > This PC.
