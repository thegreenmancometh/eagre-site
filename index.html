Stream clipper // Windows // Early access soon

Catch the moment chat goes off.

Eagre turns a long stream or VOD into vertical clips for YouTube Shorts, TikTok and Instagram Reels. It listens to the stream and reads the chat, ranks the best moments, frames the facecam, adds captions, and posts the ones you pick.

What it doesFrom a 3-hour stream to clips worth posting

01

Finds the moments

Paste a YouTube or Twitch link, or drop in a file. Eagre scores every moment on the streamer's reaction, the story and how hard the chat explodes, then lists the best first.

02

Frames it for vertical

Facecam on top and gameplay below, or a single tracked shot. It switches layout on its own when the streamer leaves the camera.

03

Captions that pop

Word-by-word captions in 25 styles with animations, names spelled right, a hook title for the first seconds, and fillers like "um" cut out.

04

Edit by deleting words

Change a clip by deleting words from its transcript. Live preview, timeline, undo, music, text boxes and sound effects.

05

Post and schedule

Send clips straight to YouTube, with TikTok and Instagram on the way. Plan them on a calendar, or export for Premiere and DaVinci.

06

Yours, on your PC

All the heavy lifting runs on your own graphics card. No upload limits, no credits per minute, and your footage stays with you.
