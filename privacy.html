Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

The short version. Eagre is a desktop app that runs on your own computer. Your videos, clips, transcripts and sign-ins stay on your computer. We don't run servers that receive your videos, we don't sell data, we don't show ads, and we don't use your content to train AI. When you ask Eagre to post a clip, it sends that clip straight from your computer to the platform you chose.

1. Who we are

Eagre ("Eagre", "we", "us") is a software product made by a small independent team. This policy covers the Eagre desktop app for Windows and this website, eagre.app. For any privacy question or request, email hello@eagre.app.

2. What stays on your computer

Eagre does its work (finding moments, transcribing speech, framing, captioning, rendering) on your own computer. The following are stored only on your computer, in Eagre's folders:

  • videos you open or download, and the clips you make;
  • transcripts, chat replays, captions, project files and settings;
  • the sign-in tokens Eagre receives when you connect a YouTube, TikTok or Instagram account.

We cannot see any of this. You can delete it at any time (see Delete your data).

4. Connected accounts

Connecting an account is optional. You sign in on the platform's own page and choose what to allow. Eagre only uses the access for the features described here, and only when you ask it to.

YouTube (Google)

  • youtube.upload: to upload the clips you choose to your own channel, with the title, description, privacy setting and time you choose.
  • youtube.readonly: to show which channel is connected (its name and picture) and the videos you have already posted.
  • yt-analytics.readonly: to show your channel's views and other statistics inside the app.

Eagre's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Google is used only to provide these features to you. It is stored only on your computer, never sent to us or anyone else, never used for advertising, and never used to train AI models. Eagre uses YouTube API Services; by connecting YouTube you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's Privacy Policy applies. You can remove Eagre's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

TikTok

  • user.info.basic: to show which TikTok account is connected (display name and avatar).
  • video.publish: to post the clips you choose to your account, with the caption, privacy level and interaction settings you choose on Eagre's posting screen.

You can remove Eagre's access in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy, Security, Apps and services permissions.

Instagram (Meta)

  • instagram_business_basic: to show which Instagram professional account is connected.
  • instagram_business_content_publish: to post the clips you choose as Reels, with the caption you choose.

Instagram only accepts videos from a web link, so when you post to Instagram, Eagre uploads that one clip to temporary private storage and gives Instagram a link that expires. The file is deleted as soon as Instagram has collected it, and in any case within 24 hours. You can remove Eagre's access in Instagram under Settings, Website permissions, Apps and websites.

5. Payments

Paid plans are sold through Polar (polar.sh), which acts as the seller and handles payment, tax and invoices. We never see your full card details. Polar shares with us the order details we need to provide your plan (such as your email address, plan and licence key). See Polar's privacy policy.

6. Emails and support

If you email us, we keep your message and email address to reply and help you. We delete support emails when they are no longer needed, and at the latest two years after the conversation ends.

7. This website

eagre.app has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking cookies. Our hosting providers may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and pages requested) for a short time to keep the site secure and running.

8. What we never do

  • Sell or rent your data.
  • Upload your videos to our own servers.
  • Use your videos, voice or channel data to train AI models.
  • Post anything without you asking Eagre to.

9. Your rights

Under data protection laws such as the UK and EU GDPR, you can ask to see, correct or delete personal data we hold about you, object to how we use it, or ask for a copy. Email hello@eagre.app and we will reply within one month. Our legal basis is providing the service you asked for (contract) and, for support emails, our legitimate interest in answering you. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

10. Children

Eagre is not meant for children under 13, and each platform you connect has its own minimum age. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

11. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top. If a change affects how your data is used, we will also say so in the app before it takes effect.
