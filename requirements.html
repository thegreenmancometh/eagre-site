PC requirements

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Eagre does all its work on your own PC, so your PC decides how fast it is. It runs on any recent Windows PC, and an NVIDIA graphics card makes it many times faster.

What you need

NeededRecommended
SystemWindows 10 or 11, 64-bitWindows 11
Graphics cardNot required (the processor can do the work, slowly)NVIDIA GeForce GTX 10 series or newer; RTX 20 series or newer is fastest
Memory (RAM)8 GB16 GB or more
Free disk spaceAbout 25 GB for Eagre and its downloads50 GB or more, so long streams and finished clips have room
InternetFor the first-start download (up to about 15 GB) and for pasting stream linksA fast connection makes the first start quicker

Mac and Linux are not supported.

How fast will it be?

The slow part is "finding the moments": Eagre listens to the whole stream and reads the chat. Making a clip after that takes a minute or less on most PCs. Times below are for finding the moments in a one-hour stream, measured on our own test PCs:

Your PCOne-hour streamWhat Eagre says
NVIDIA RTX graphics card (for example an RTX 3060 Ti)about 5 minutesFast
Older NVIDIA card (GTX 10 series)a few minutes longer than RTXFast or Okay
No NVIDIA card, a recent processor (for example an Intel Core i5-14400F)about 30 to 35 minutesOkay
Older or laptop processors without an NVIDIA cardcan be over 45 minutesSlow

On a slow PC Eagre still works: start a long stream before bed and the moments are ready in the morning, or clip shorter recordings.

Check your own PC

Eagre measures your PC instead of guessing. The first time you open it, it runs a one-minute speed check. You can run it again any time in Settings > This PC > Check speed. It shows your processor, graphics card, memory and free space, how fast each part runs, and gives one of three verdicts:

  • Fast: a one-hour stream is scanned in 15 minutes or less.
  • Okay: up to 45 minutes. Start a long stream and come back later.
  • Slow: more than 45 minutes. Scan overnight, or clip shorter recordings.

Graphics cards

  • NVIDIA: the best choice. Eagre uses the card for speech-to-text, the AI and video.
  • AMD and Intel graphics: Eagre uses them to save video, but speech-to-text and the AI run on the processor, so finding moments is slower. These cards are less tested; please tell us if something doesn't work.
  • No graphics card: everything runs on the processor. It works, just slower.
