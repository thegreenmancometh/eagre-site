Setting up Eagre

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Short version: run the installer, open Eagre, let the first start download its parts (20 to 60 minutes, once), then follow the tour to make a clip from the sample stream.

1. Install Eagre

Before you start, check that your PC can run Eagre: see PC requirements. You need Windows 10 or 11 and about 25 GB of free space.

  1. Download Eagre-Setup.exe (about 50 MB) and open it.
  2. Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" or say the publisher is unknown. Click More info, then Run anyway. (This happens because the installer isn't signed with a paid certificate yet. It doesn't mean anything is wrong.)
  3. Follow the installer. It doesn't need an administrator password: Eagre installs just for your Windows user and puts an Eagre icon on your desktop and in the Start menu.

2. The first start

Eagre runs its AI on your own PC instead of on our servers, so the first time you open it, it downloads the parts it needs. A progress page opens in your browser and shows each part:

PartWhat it doesSize
App toolboxThe engine that makes the clips, set up for your graphics cardabout 3.5 GB
Speech-to-text toolboxListens to the stream to find moments and write captionsabout 3 GB
FFmpegCuts and saves video (skipped if your PC already has it)about 0.1 GB
Speech-to-text modelsThe models that turn speech into wordsabout 0.9 GB
Voice separatorTakes music and game sound out before listeningabout 0.3 GB
Writing AI (optional)Picks moments and writes hooks and titles; you can skip itabout 6.6 GB

In total that is up to about 15 GB and usually takes 20 to 60 minutes, depending on your internet.

  • You can do something else while it runs.
  • If you close Eagre or lose your connection, nothing is lost: the next time you open Eagre it carries on where it stopped.
  • If a part fails, the page shows why, with a Try again button. Most failures are a dropped internet connection. See Common problems if it keeps failing.

After this, the Eagre icon opens the app in a few seconds. A small black window opens too (minimised): that window is Eagre itself, so leave it open while you use the app. Closing it closes Eagre.

The first time the app opens, it also runs a one-minute speed check of your PC. You can see the result, and run it again, in Settings > This PC.

3. Make your first clip

A short tour starts the first time you open the app. Its last step opens a sample stream that is already scanned, so you can make a clip in a couple of minutes. To clip your own stream:

  1. Paste a stream. Paste a Twitch, YouTube or Kick link, or drop a video file from your PC.
  2. Pick a recipe. A recipe sets the caption style, layout and clip length in one click. You can change everything later.
  3. Find the best moments. Eagre listens to the whole stream and reads the chat, then lists the best moments with a score and the reason. A one-hour stream takes about 5 minutes on a fast PC and longer on a slower one (see PC requirements).
  4. Open a moment in the editor to trim it, fix words, change the captions or the framing. Or tick several moments and make them all at once.
  5. Make the clip. Finished clips appear under Clips, ready to download or post. They are also saved in a folder on your PC (Settings shows which one).

Want new streams scanned without doing anything? Add the streamer under Channels. While Eagre is open, it scans their new streams so the moments are waiting for you.

4. Connect YouTube for posting

Posting is optional: you can always download a clip and upload it yourself. To post straight from Eagre, YouTube needs a one-time setup with a free Google account. It takes about 10 minutes, and you only do it once per PC.

  1. Go to console.cloud.google.com and sign in with the Google account that owns your channel.
  2. Create a new project (any name, for example "Eagre").
  3. In APIs & Services > Library, search for YouTube Data API v3 and click Enable.
  4. In APIs & Services > OAuth consent screen, set it up as External, fill in an app name and your email, and add your own Google account as a test user.
  5. In APIs & Services > Credentials, click Create credentials > OAuth client ID, choose Desktop app, then download the JSON file.
  6. In Eagre, open a finished clip and click Post. Open the JSON file in Notepad, copy everything in it, paste it into the box and click Save.
  7. Click Link a YouTube channel and sign in with Google. Pick the channel you post clips to.

Your sign-in is stored only on your PC. You can see and disconnect linked channels in Settings > Connected accounts.

5. TikTok and Instagram

Posting to TikTok and Instagram is being finished. Until then, download the clip from Clips and upload it in the TikTok or Instagram app. This guide will show the steps once direct posting is ready.

6. Unlock a paid plan

Eagre works on the free plan with limits. Your plan and what is left this month are shown in Settings > Your plan. After you buy a plan you get a licence key by email: paste it into Settings > Licence key and click Unlock. One key works on 2 PCs (for example a desktop and a laptop).

7. Updates

Open Settings > Updates and click Check for updates. It shows what's new; click Download update, then Install and restart. Your settings, licence key, projects and clips are kept.
